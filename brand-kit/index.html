Part 1

Brand kaise develop karein

Brand ka matlab sirf logo nahi. Log Taazu ke baare mein kya sochte hain, woh har jagah ek jaisa dikhne, bolne aur kaam karne se banta hai. Yeh 7 steps usi order mein karo.

Step 1 · Positioning statement
Amdavad mein jo log garmi mein khelte ya kaam karte hain, unke liye Taazu ek desi nimbu-namak electrolyte drink hai jo paseene mein gaya namak wapas deta hai. Cold drink jitna easy, pocket-friendly, ghar ke nimbu-paani jaisa taste.

Brand ka core

Naam
Taazu · તાજું · "fresh"
Ek line
Amdavad ka apna electrolyte drink.
Kyun maanein
Asli nimbu aur namak, potassium, NABL lab-tested label, FSSAI licence, Ahmedabad mein bana.
Personality
Desi, cheerful, seedhi baat. Ek samajhdar dost jo coach bhi hai.
Tagline
Paani se aage.

Step 2 · Kiske liye (priority order)

1Gym + box cricket, 18–35 2Runners, cyclists 3Site aur factory workers 4Delivery riders, students 5Navratri garba (seasonal)

Pehle 1 aur 2 par focus karo: turfs, gyms aur clubs ek jagah bahut log dete hain, aur woh jaldi dusron ko batate hain. Workers tak contractor ya canteen ke through pahuncho.

Voice: aise bolo

  • ✓Hinglish, aur Gujarati words jahan natural lage
  • ✓Chhote sentences, ek post mein ek baat
  • ✓Har fact ke saath source ki chhoti line
  • ✓Asli Amdavadi jagah aur log

Voice: yeh kabhi nahi

  • ✕"ORS" word, kisi bhi tarah (FSSAI rules)
  • ✕Ilaaj, bachav, cure, "doctor recommended", "health drink"
  • ✕"Low sugar" jab tak lab report na ho
  • ✕Heatstroke ka darr dikha kar bechna

Step 4 · Identity lock karo

Logo

Concept A (Nimbu Drop) primary. Seal cups aur stickers ke liye. Neeche Part 2 dekho.

Fonts

Baloo Bhai 2 headlines (English + Gujarati), Baloo 2 Hindi, Plus Jakarta Sans body. Sab Canva mein free.

Graphics

Boond, nimbu slice, suraj. Photos mein dhoop, paseena, turfs, pol ki galiyan. Ek creative mein ek hi strong background colour.

Step 5 · Launch roadmap

Week 1–2Foundation

Logo final karo. IP India par class 32 mein "Taazu" search karke trademark file karo (₹4,500, proprietor/MSME). Instagram, WhatsApp Business aur Google Business Profile same naam se. Launch se pehle 9 posts ready rakho taaki profile khali na lage.

Week 3–6Sampling

Gyms aur turfs par A4 standee + free tasting. QR se survey bharwao. 50+ reviews aur photos collect karo (customer ki permission lekar). Jo cheez log baar-baar bolein, wahi agla post.

OctoberNavratri

Garba grounds par Taazu station. "Garba ki raat lambi hai" campaign, roz reels. 5–10 local micro-creators (5–10k followers, food/fitness) ke saath barter.

Nov–FebOff-season

Sardi mein gyms, cricket academies, marathons aur corporate canteens. WhatsApp reorder list banao, repeat customers ko pehle naya flavour chakhao.

Mar–JunSummer peak

Site workers, delivery riders, bazaar. Gujarati heat campaign "ગરમીમાં પણ તાજું". Yahi asli season hai, stock aur stations pehle se ready rakho.

Step 6 · Content ke 5 pillars

P1Jo log nahi jaante

Sourced facts ki series: paseena, 2%, pyaas late.

P2Amdavad ki garmi

Local temperature, pol, riverfront, Gujarati lines.

P3Khiladi aur kaamgar

Asli customers ki photos aur chhote videos.

P4Behind the scenes

Lab test, label printing, co-packer visit, pehla batch.

P5Kahan milega

Stations, gyms, offers, WhatsApp order.

RhythmHar hafte

3 posts + 1 reel + roz WhatsApp Status. Har post par logo same corner mein.

Step 7 · Kya measure karein

Followers se zyada yeh 3 numbers dekho: samples ke baad dobara khareedne wale, survey mein "would buy" %, aur WhatsApp par reorder messages. Sab Taazu app ke Survey aur Sales tab mein log karo.

Part 5

AI image prompts

Midjourney, Ideogram, ChatGPT, Gemini, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo aur video tools (Veo, Kling, Runway) ke liye. Sab English mein hain kyunki yeh tools English prompts sabse achhe samajhte hain.

Pehle yeh padho

  • AI se sirf photo ya background banwao. Headline, logo aur source Canva mein lagao, kyunki AI Hindi aur Gujarati text bigaad deta hai.
  • Har prompt ke end mein brand style line jodo, taaki saari images ek family jaisi lagein.
  • Asli bottle dikhana ho to ChatGPT ya Gemini mein bottle ki photo upload karke A3 wala prompt use karo. AI ko label dobara mat banane do.
  • Log asli dikhein: Indian faces, Amdavadi jagah. Workers ko respect ke saath dikhao.

Brand style line

warm sunlit palette of saffron orange (#EA580C), lemon yellow (#F5D83B), fresh leaf green (#1F7A3A) and cream (#FFF8E7), clean commercial photography, natural Indian skin tones, realistic, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermark

Negative prompt (Leonardo, Firefly, SD): text, letters, logo, watermark, extra fingers, distorted hands, plastic skin, oversaturated, cartoonish, western setting
