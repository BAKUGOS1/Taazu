# Taazu Brand Kit

Taazu (તાજું, Gujarati for "fresh") is a still nimbu-namak electrolyte drink, 250 ml, launching as a pilot in Ahmedabad. This folder has everything needed to build the brand: strategy, logo files, slogans, ready creatives and AI image prompts.

| Folder / file | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| `logos/` | 4 logo concepts, each as icon-only and with name, in SVG (print) and PNG (social). Text is converted to shapes, so the SVGs open correctly anywhere without the font. |
| `creatives/` | 8 ready awareness creatives: 5 Instagram posts (1080×1350), 1 story (1080×1920), 1 A4 counter poster (300 dpi), 1 Navratri post. |
| `AI-PROMPTS.md` | Prompts for Midjourney, Ideogram, ChatGPT, Gemini, Firefly and video tools, plus captions for each creative. |

Claims rule for everything below: keep hydration facts general and sourced. Never call Taazu "ORS", never say it treats, prevents or cures anything, and don't write "low sugar" until the NABL lab report supports it.

## 1. Brand kaise develop karein

### Step 1: Brand ka core likho (ek baar, phir har jagah wahi)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Naam** | Taazu · તાજું · "fresh" |
| **Ek line mein** | Amdavad ka apna electrolyte drink. |
| **Positioning** | Amdavad mein jo log garmi mein khelte ya kaam karte hain, unke liye Taazu ek desi nimbu-namak electrolyte drink hai jo paseene mein gaya namak wapas deta hai. Cold drink jitna easy, pocket-friendly, aur ghar ke nimbu-paani jaisa taste. |
| **Kyun maanein** | Asli nimbu aur namak, potassium, NABL lab-tested nutrition label, FSSAI licence, Ahmedabad mein bana. |
| **Personality** | Desi, cheerful, seedhi baat. Ek samajhdar dost jo coach bhi hai. |
| **Tagline** | Paani se aage. (More than water.) |

### Step 2: Audience ko priority mein rakho

1. Gym aur box-cricket players (18–35), turfs aur academies ke through
2. Runners aur cycling clubs
3. Site aur factory workers, contractors aur canteens ke through
4. Delivery riders, students aur summer mein bahar rehne wale log
5. Navratri garba players (seasonal)

### Step 3: Competition mein apni jagah pakdo

| Option | Log kyun lete hain | Taazu ka angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ORS (pharmacy) | Beemari mein | Taazu daily drink hai, dawai nahi. ORS word kabhi use mat karo. |
| Glucon-D / Tang type powder | Sasta, sweet | Taazu ready-to-drink hai, sugar-first nahi, salt-first. |
| Gatorade type sports drinks | Gym image | Desi taste, local price, Amdavadi brand. |
| Nimbu soda / shikanji thela | Sasta, taste | Sealed, har baar same taste, label par sab likha. |
| Cold drinks | Thanda, habit | Sirf sugar nahi, namak bhi wapas. |

### Step 4: Identity lock karo

- **Logo:** Concept A (Nimbu Drop) primary. Seal secondary, cups aur stickers ke liye. Details section 2 mein.
- **Colours:** Taazu Orange `#EA580C`, Nimbu `#F5D83B`, Leaf `#1F7A3A`, Cream `#FFF8E7`, Ink `#1C1917`. Ek creative mein ek hi strong background colour.
- **Fonts:** Baloo Bhai 2 ExtraBold for headlines (Latin + Gujarati), Baloo 2 for Hindi, Plus Jakarta Sans for body text. Sab Google Fonts par free hain aur Canva mein bhi milte hain.
- **Graphics:** paani ki boond, nimbu slice, suraj. Photos mein asli Amdavadi log, dhoop, paseena, turfs, pol ki galiyan.
- **Voice:** Hinglish + Gujarati words, chhote sentences, har fact ke saath source. Darr mat failao, medical baat mat karo.

### Step 5: Launch roadmap

| Phase | Kab | Kya karna hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Week 1–2 | Logo final, IP India par class 32 trademark search + filing (₹4,500), Instagram + WhatsApp Business + Google Business Profile same naam se, bio, 9 posts ready before launch. |
| Sampling | Week 3–6 | Gyms/turfs par A4 standee + free tasting, QR survey, 50+ reviews aur photos collect karo (customer ki permission ke saath). |
| Navratri | October | Garba grounds par Taazu station, reels, 5–10 local micro-creators (barter), "Garba ki raat lambi hai" campaign. |
| Off-season | Nov–Feb | Gyms, academies, marathons, corporate canteens. WhatsApp reorder list banao. |
| Summer peak | Mar–Jun | Site workers, delivery riders, Gujarati heat campaign "ગરમીમાં પણ તાજું". |

### Step 6: Content ke 5 pillars

1. **Jo log nahi jaante:** sourced facts (paseena, 2%, pyaas late). Series format.
2. **Amdavad ki garmi:** local temperature, pol, riverfront, Gujarati lines.
3. **Khiladi aur kaamgar:** asli customers ki photos/videos (permission lekar).
4. **Behind the scenes:** lab testing, labels, co-packer visit, pehla batch.
5. **Kahan milega:** stations, gyms, offers, WhatsApp order.

Rhythm: har hafte 3 posts + 1 reel + roz WhatsApp Status. Har post par same logo corner aur same colours.

### Step 7: Kya measure karein

Followers se zyada yeh dekho: samples diye → kitne ne dobara khareeda, survey mein "would buy" %, WhatsApp par reorder messages. Sab Taazu app ke Survey aur Sales tab mein log karo.

## 2. Logo concepts

| Concept | Icon (without name) | With name | Idea | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A · Nimbu Drop** (recommended) | `concept-A-icon-color` | `concept-A-horizontal-color`, `concept-A-stacked-color` | Paani ki boond ke andar nimbu slice: drink + nimbu ek nazar mein. | Label, cap, WhatsApp DP, sab kuch. Chhote size par bhi saaf. |
| **B · Sun Drop** | `concept-B-icon-color` | `concept-B-horizontal-color`, `concept-B-stacked-color` | Suraj ke saamne thandi boond: "garmi ka jawab". | Summer campaigns, banners. Chhote size par details kho jaati hain. |
| **C · t-wave monogram** | `concept-C-icon-color` | `concept-C-horizontal-color`, `concept-C-stacked-color` | Lowercase "t" ki crossbar ek lehar, saath mein nimbu-yellow boond. | App icon, Instagram DP, merch. |
| **D · Anusvara wordmark** | (name-only) | `concept-D-wordmark-color` | તાજું mein "ં" ka dot boond ban kar "u" ke upar aa gaya. | Banners, lambi labels, jahan icon ki jagah nahi. |
| **Seal** | `seal-color`, `seal-reverse` | | "Taazu · Amdavad · Paani se aage" round stamp. | Cup sleeves, stickers, cap-top, delivery bags. |

Every concept has a `-reverse` version for orange or dark backgrounds, and `concept-A-horizontal-cream-transparent` for placing on photos.

Rules: logo ke charon taraf kam se kam "a" letter ki height jitni khali jagah. Print mein icon 12 mm se chhota nahi. Stretch, shadow, outline ya naye colour mat lagao. Photo par logo lagana ho to orange block ya cream-transparent version use karo.

Before printing: final logo ko ek designer se clean karwao aur IP India par trademark file karo.

## 3. Slogans

**Main tagline:** Paani se aage. · पानी से आगे। · More than water.
**Sign-off / hashtag:** #TaazuRaho
**Gujarati line:** ગરમીમાં પણ તાજું. (Garmi ma pan taazu: fresh, even in the heat.)

**Hinglish options**
- Jo paseene mein gaya, woh wapas.
- Pyaas se pehle, Taazu.
- Garmi ka desi jawab.
- Nimbu, namak, aur thoda science.
- Amdavad ka apna electrolyte.

**Gujarati options**
- ગરમીમાં પણ તાજું. (Garmi ma pan taazu.)
- અમદાવાદનું પોતાનું. (Amdavad nu potanu.)
- પરસેવો ગયો? તાજું લો. (Parsevo gayo? Taazu lo.)

**English options**
- More than water.
- Put back what the heat takes.
- Fresh, even at 44°C.

**Campaign lines**
- Navratri: Garba ki raat lambi hai.
- Box cricket: Last over tak taazu.
- Gym: Set ke beech, ek sip Taazu.
- Site workers: Kaam lamba, dhoop tez. Taazu saath.

**Kabhi mat likho:** "ORS jaisa", "dehydration ka ilaaj", "heatstroke se bachaye", "doctor recommended", "health drink".
