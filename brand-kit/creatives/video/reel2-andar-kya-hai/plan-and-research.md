# Taazu video #2: product-only reel (plan, 30 Sep 2026)

Rule from mohit: no people, no cartoon figures, nothing that can look "broken". Only the real bottle renders, real-looking ingredients, the logo, and type.

## What the research says (Reels ads, 2026)
- First 3 seconds decide the scroll, and frame 1 must already be moving (object entering, text animating). Static openers get skipped.
- 15 to 30 s works best; beyond 60 s completion drops. Structure: hook 0-3 s, problem 4-10 s, product 11-20 s, CTA in the last 5-10 s.
- Audio matters (music/sound lifts engagement ~35%), but design so it still works on mute: big on-screen words.
- Safe zone on 1080x1920: keep text away from the top ~270 px and the bottom ~670 px, and ~120 px off the right edge (Like/Comment buttons). In video #1 the bottom chips sat in the unsafe zone; video #2 will keep everything in the middle.
- Show the product clearly and early; one reveal per beat, not clutter.
Sources: digitalapplied.com (Reels ads creative guide 2026), zeely.ai (Instagram safe zones 2026), adlibrary.com (Reels ads 2026).

## Concept options
**A. "Andar kya hai?" (ingredient build) ⭐**
Real ingredients drop into a clean cream frame one by one with a satisfying beat: nimbu slice → namak crystals → jeera → pudina → ice. Each lands with one bold word. Then they swirl into the glossy drop, the drop splashes, and the bottle rises. Ends with both flavours + CTA. Feels premium, 100% product, no chance of broken figures.

**B. "44° se thanda" (heat to chill)**
Orange heat screen with a thermometer at 44°, a frosty bottle slides in, frost spreads across the screen, number drops, condensation drips down the label macro-style. Simple and very "Amdavad summer".

**C. "Team Nimbu ya Team Jeera?" (flavour battle)**
Split screen, both bottles face off with their ingredients, quick stat cards, ends with "Comment karo: Nimbu ya Jeera?" Good for comments/engagement.
