# Taazu reel 2 "Andar kya hai?" : Gemini storyboard image prompts (30 Sep 2026)

Kaise use karein:
- Har prompt alag se Gemini mein paste karo. Har frame ke saath apni **bottle render (Classic ya Jeera)** reference image ke roop mein attach karo, taaki bottle same dikhe.
- Sab frames 9:16 vertical hain. Koi insaan, haath, cartoon ya mascot nahi.
- Text Gemini se mat likhwao (spelling bigad jaati hai). Words hum video mein upar se daalte hain. Isliye har prompt mein "no text" hai.
- Upar ka ~15% aur neeche ka ~35% hissa khaali/simple rakho (Instagram buttons wahan aate hain).
