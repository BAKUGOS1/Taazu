# Taazu: product concept + motion video plan (draft, 29 Sep 2026)

## 1. Concept: "Garmi ka Reset"
Amdavad ki garmi, garba ke 3 ghante, site pe dhoop: body thak jaati hai, sirf paani se "reset" nahi hota.
Taazu = ek ghoont mein **desi taste + electrolytes**, bina fizz ke. Nimbu Namak aur Jeera Masala, jo ghar ka swaad hai, ab ek chilled 250 ml bottle mein.

- Hero idea: **the drop**. Logo wali glossy paani ki boond hi story ka hero hai. Garmi mein boond girti hai, splash hota hai, aur usme se Taazu bottle nikalti hai.
- Feel: bright, juicy, fast cuts on beat. Colours: cream, orange, yellow, green (Nimbu), brown (Jeera).
- Copy rules: "ORS" word nahi; tagline ke alawa koi health claim nahi ("Rehydrate | Refresh | Recover" hi).

## 2. Storyboard (15 s version, 9:16)
| Time | Visual | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | Orange screen, heat shimmer, sun pulsing, thermometer shoots up | "Amdavad. 44°" |
| 2.0–3.5 | Screen "cracks" dry; one glossy water drop falls from top, slow-mo | "Paseena gaya…" |
| 3.5–5.5 | Drop hits, big splash; lemon slice, ice cube, mint leaf fly out | "…Taazu aaya" |
| 5.5–8.0 | Classic Nimbu Namak bottle (orange cap) rises out of splash, turns | "Classic Nimbu Namak" |
| 8.0–10.0 | Whip-pan; Jeera Masala bottle (brown cap), jeera seeds burst | "Jeera Masala" |
| 10.0–12.5 | Both bottles side by side; three words punch in on the beat | "Rehydrate · Refresh · Recover" |
| 12.5–15.0 | TAAZU logo lockup on cream, drop shines | "Non-carbonated · Desi taste" + CTA |

30 s version = same beats plus a Navratri garba scene (dandiya, lights) and a gym/turf scene before the splash.
