# Taazu "Garmi ka Reset": AI video prompts (Veo / Kling / Runway)

Use these if you want a cinematic, real-looking version. Tip: give the AI a start frame (Gemini image from /mnt/project-files/website/assets/references/, e.g. 12-both-flavours-duo.jpg) so the bottle and label stay correct. AI tools often garble label text, so keep label close-ups short and add all on-screen text later in CapCut/Instagram.

Settings for every shot: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 4 to 6 seconds, no on-screen text, no people's faces unless stated, no logos other than the bottle label.

## Shot 1: Heat (0 to 3 s)
Cinematic slow-motion shot of an Ahmedabad street at noon in peak summer, harsh orange sunlight, visible heat haze shimmering above the road, sun flare in the top of the frame, warm orange and yellow colour grade, dusty air, a tired person wiping sweat from their forehead in soft focus in the background. Camera slowly pushes in. Photorealistic, commercial film look.

## Shot 2: The drop (3 to 5 s)
Extreme macro, super slow motion: a single crystal-clear glossy water drop containing a tiny lemon slice, a small ice cube and a mint leaf falls through warm orange light against a blurred orange background. Soft highlights and refractions on the drop surface. Camera tracks the drop downward. Photorealistic, 1000 fps look.

## Shot 3: Splash (5 to 7 s)
The water drop hits a still surface and explodes into a big crown splash in super slow motion; lemon slices, ice cubes and fresh mint leaves burst outward through the splash; the background changes from orange to soft cream (#FFF8E7). Bright, fresh, juicy commercial look.

## Shot 4: Classic bottle reveal (7 to 10 s)
A chilled 250 ml clear PET bottle with an orange cap and a cream label with a yellow band (use the reference image for the label) rises up out of the splash and rotates slowly to face the camera. Cold condensation droplets on the bottle, pale lemon-coloured still (non-carbonated) drink inside, lemon slices and mint leaves floating around it, yellow sunburst rays behind on cream. Studio product lighting.

## Shot 5: Jeera Masala bottle (10 to 12 s)
Fast whip-pan to the second bottle: same 250 ml PET shape with a brown cap and a cream label with a brown band (use reference image 11-jeera-masala-a2.jpg), light amber still drink inside, roasted cumin seeds bursting around the bottle in slow motion, warm brown background with soft light rays. Studio product lighting, condensation on bottle.

## Shot 6: Duo hero (12 to 15 s)
Both bottles (orange cap Classic Nimbu Namak on the left, brown cap Jeera Masala on the right) standing on a bed of crushed ice and rock salt crystals, lemon halves and mint around them, bright orange gradient background, slow orbit camera move, fresh ice mist rising. Premium beverage commercial. Leave empty space at the top of the frame for text.
